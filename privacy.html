Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

Celengan (https://rf-dev11.github.io/tankigoblin/)

1. Overview (Ringkasan)

Celengan is a personal finance tracking application designed so that your financial data stays exclusively in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a backend database that stores your transactions, account balances, or personal spreadsheets.

(Celengan adalah aplikasi catatan keuangan pribadi yang dirancang agar seluruh data keuangan Anda tersimpan langsung di Google Drive Anda sendiri. Kami tidak menyimpan data transaksi, saldo, maupun spreadsheet Anda di server kami.)

2. Google Account Data & Scopes Accessed

When you sign in with Google, Celengan requests only the minimum OAuth permissions required to function:

  • openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile: Used solely to identify your Google account, display your email address inside the app, and keep your session linked to your spreadsheet.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Used strictly to create, locate, read, and update the single dedicated spreadsheet named "Celengan - Catatan Keuangan" created by this app in your Google Drive. Celengan cannot view, edit, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

3. How Your Data Is Stored & Used

  • Your Google Drive: All financial records (wallets, categories, transactions, budgets, assets, debts, recurring items, goals, and net-worth history) are saved directly to your own Google Spreadsheet via official Google Drive & Google Sheets APIs.
  • Local Browser Storage: App preferences (color theme, card order/design, and temporary session token) are stored locally on your device using localStorage and sessionStorage.
  • No Selling or Third-Party Sharing: We never sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data or financial data with third parties or advertisers.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Celengan's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion (Hapus Data & Cabut Akses)

You are in full control of your data at all times:

  • Sign out from the app via Akun → Pengaturan → Keluar akun Google to clear local session data on your device.
  • Delete the "Celengan - Catatan Keuangan" file directly from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Revoke Celengan's access to your Google Account anytime through Google Account Permissions.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
